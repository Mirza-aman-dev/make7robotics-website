# Make7 Robotics — Website Content Architecture

> **Purpose:** Content and information architecture for the new Make7 Robotics website.
>
> **Source basis:** Current Make7 Robotics website content + supplied Make7 Robotics business plan.
>
> **Scope rule:** This document defines **what the website should communicate, in what order, and what content belongs in each section**. It does **not** prescribe the visual design system, layout styling, animation style, colors, typography, or component aesthetics.
>
> **Design instruction:** For all UI/UX, visual direction, responsive behavior, interaction design, component styling, and design-system decisions, use the **`ui-ux-pro-max` skill from the project's `skills` folder** and follow the UI prompt supplied with that skill.
>
> **Important:** Do not replace, reinterpret, or invent business claims that are not supported by the supplied source material. Where the source uses terms such as “proprietary software”, “accredited diploma pathways”, “advanced artificial intelligence engine”, “enterprise grade security”, or “perfectly match official school technology and artificial intelligence goals”, verify the actual operational/legal basis before publishing them as factual claims.

---

## 1. Website Strategy

### 1.1 Primary website objective

Position Make7 Robotics as a Qatar-focused education technology partner that helps schools introduce practical technology education through a turnkey model that combines:

- Existing school computer infrastructure
- Make7 learning software
- Make7 expert IT teachers
- Curriculum delivery
- Student progress tracking
- Parent/principal visibility
- Hackathons and ideation activities
- Long-term progression into physical robotics

The website must communicate that Make7 is **not simply selling robotics kits**. The core proposition is a **school-integrated learning ecosystem** designed to make advanced technology education accessible, measurable, and operationally simple for schools.

### 1.2 Primary audience

**Decision-makers at schools in Qatar**

Examples:
- School owners
- Principals
- Academic leaders
- Technology/ICT leaders
- School management and board decision-makers

### 1.3 Secondary audiences

**Parents**

They need to understand:
- Why the program matters for their children
- What students actually learn
- Why it does not require evening/weekend travel to external centers
- How progress can be tracked
- Why the program is affordable

**Students**

They should see:
- Coding and technology as practical and exciting
- A path from logic to coding to AI to real robotics
- Opportunities for projects, hackathons, ideation, and achievement

### 1.4 Primary conversion

The dominant conversion should be:

**School / institution partnership inquiry**

Primary CTA language should remain aligned with the supplied brand language, such as:
- Partner With Us
- Let's Innovate Together
- Ready to empower your students?
- Send Inquiry

### 1.5 Secondary conversions

- Contact Make7 Robotics
- Request information / support plan
- Explore the program
- Explore the learning journey
